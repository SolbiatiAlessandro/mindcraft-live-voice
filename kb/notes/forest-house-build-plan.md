FOREST STARTER CABIN — Badcock + a16i

Recommendation
Build this simplified oak-and-cobblestone cabin, not a large cottage: 7 x 7 foundation, 5 x 5 usable interior, three-block-high walls, flat 9 x 9 bottom-slab roof with one-block eaves. Total height including foundation is 4.5 blocks. This is an original coordinate plan inspired by online starter-house designs, not a copied tutorial; the flat roof avoids stair orientation and a costly loft. Oak/birch variants can substitute consistently. No glass, iron, or smelting is required for the shell.

Exact placed-material list
  24 cobblestone — foundation perimeter
  25 oak planks — interior floor
  52 oak planks — walls, excluding posts/windows/door
  12 oak logs — four three-high corner posts
  81 oak slabs — roof, all bottom-half slabs
   6 oak fences — three two-wide barred windows
   1 oak door — occupies two vertical block positions
   2 beds — four floor positions total
   2 chests — adjacent as a double chest
   1 crafting table
   1 furnace
   8 torches — four inside, four outside

Gather/craft budget
53 oak logs, 32 cobblestone, 6 wool (three matching-color wool per bed), and 2 coal. Keep 12 logs intact; convert 41 to 164 planks. Spend 77 planks on floor/walls, 42 on 84 slabs, 8 plus 4 sticks on 6 fences, 6 on 3 doors, 4 on the table, 16 on chests, 6 on beds, and 4 on 8 sticks. Two sticks plus the coal make 8 torches. Furnace uses the remaining 8 cobblestone. Leftovers: 1 plank, 3 slabs, 2 doors, 2 sticks. Tools, food, temporary dirt scaffolding, terrain repairs, and any fuel needed to make charcoal instead of finding coal are EXTRA; budget assumes coal and existing tools. Gathering 64 logs instead gives a practical spare margin.

Coordinate build sequence
All ranges inclusive. Origin (0,0,0) is the front-left foundation corner; x goes right, z goes toward the back, y goes up. Translate to a clear, level site with the foundation flush with surrounding ground. Clear the entire roof footprint x,z=-1..7 and overhead branches.

1. Badcock gathers wood/crafts while a16i gathers stone, coal, and wool; meet at the chosen site before night. If wool is unavailable, finish the shelter first and add beds later.
2. At y=0, fill x,z=0..6: cobblestone around the perimeter, planks in x,z=1..5. This is exactly 24 cobblestone + 25 planks.
3. At (x,z)=(0,0),(6,0),(0,6),(6,6), stack logs y=1..3. Fill the remaining perimeter at y=1..3 with planks EXCEPT the door at (3,1,0)/(3,2,0) and windows listed below. Place one door in that opening; leave the plank lintel at (3,3,0).
4. Put fences at (0,2,2),(0,2,3),(6,2,2),(6,2,3),(2,2,6),(3,2,6). These fill every window opening without needing sand; they are not arrow-proof glazing.
5. Cover every x,z=-1..7 at y=4 with exactly one BOTTOM slab per position. Start from wall tops, work inward and outward using temporary dirt access, then remove it. Do not double slabs or use top slabs: bottom slabs prevent normal mob spawning on the roof [3].
6. Furnish at y=1: beds along z=4..5 at x=1 and x=2 (heads at z=5); chests at (4,1,5),(5,1,5); table (5,1,1); furnace (5,1,2). Keep the center aisle and space above chests clear.
7. Floor torches at (1,1,1),(4,1,1),(1,1,3),(4,1,3). Exterior ground torches at x,z=(1,-1),(5,-1),(1,7),(5,7), at the air block above the local ground. Keep the door shut; fence windows and a wooden door are not a fortress. Hard-difficulty zombies can break wooden doors. Stay away from fire/lava; wood is flammable.

Acceptance check
Walk the entrance and center aisle, open both chests, access both beds, check every wall opening is filled and all 81 roof slabs are bottom-half. Check lighting in-game, including the surrounding approach; do not assume four outside torches secure the entire area. Coordinate enumeration in Python verified the shell counts; this plan has NOT been built or tested in-game.
